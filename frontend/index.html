Connect with any standard EVM wallet (browser extension or wallet app browser). You always review and confirm the check-in transaction in your own wallet.

How it works

AgentCheck reads the Agent ID registry on BNB Chain: if your address holds an Agent ID, you can check in once per UTC day. Consecutive days build your streak. The contract has no owner and no admin functions, holds no funds, and never asks for token approvals — other dApps can read your streak and check-in history straight from the chain.

Check-ins are free except the BNB Chain network fee (gas) paid to the network. AgentCheck is a reputation record only — it issues no token and promises no returns or rewards. Contract: not deployed yet
